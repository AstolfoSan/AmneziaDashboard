# Community guidelines

Be respectful, constructive and patient. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing and disclosure of another person's private information are not accepted.
Discuss technical choices without personal attacks. Do not publish credentials
or encourage testing on systems you do not own or have permission to use.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content and restrict participation where
needed. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub
profile; do not post the affected person's private information in an issue.

Общайтесь уважительно. Оскорбления, угрозы, травля и публикация чужих личных
данных недопустимы. Проверяйте только свои системы или системы с разрешением
владельца. О проблемах сообщайте владельцу проекта приватно.
